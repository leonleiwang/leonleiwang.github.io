Current interests

Questions I keep coming back to.

These questions come up in my engineering work, and I’d like to explore them further.

  1. 01

    When should an agent hand a decision back to a person?

    How can tool permissions, context, and review points make that handoff clear?

  2. 02

    How can people trace a generated answer back to evidence?

    I’m interested in retrieval, citations, and ways to show uncertainty when sources are incomplete.

  3. 03

    How do we understand what an agent did?

    Evaluation, traces, and replay can help explain unexpected behavior and show whether a change improved it.
